# About

I am Ritik Agarwal, a smart contract security researcher based in Ahmedabad, India. My work combines public competitive audits, private security reviews, and security engineering.

I started in software development, moved into Solidity and DApps, then shifted to security after becoming interested in protocol attack surfaces and EVM behavior. That path now informs how I approach an audit: understand the system's intended behavior, model how value and permissions move through it, and prove impact with a clear reproduction.

Outside audits, I am co-building Shim, a Go-based package-manager security tool, and studying zero-knowledge proofs and cryptography.
